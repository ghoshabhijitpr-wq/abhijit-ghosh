About SAMA

Smart Addressal & Management of Accounts

SAMA is Emami Agrotech Ltd.'s proprietary complaint management and customer support platform. Built to streamline complaint registration, tracking, and resolution across our extensive product and distribution network — spanning sunflower, mustard, soybean, rice bran, groundnut oils and more. The embedded AI assistant EMA provides 24/7 support to customers. All complaint data is stored locally for privacy; exports are available in JSON and Excel formats.
