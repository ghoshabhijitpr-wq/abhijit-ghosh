COPQ Reduction — Emami Agrotech

MBA Dissertation · Haldia Plant (M251) & Dhulagarh CFA (D257) · March–May 2026

📌 Full Title

Cost of Poor Quality (COPQ) Reduction in Logistics Operations: A Pan-India Study of Wastages Across the Distribution Network of Emami Agrotech Limited — FY 2025–26

Guide: Mr. Debanjan Chakravarti, Manager – CS&L, Emami Agrotech Ltd. | Exam Roll No. 240018

🎯 Problem

On a peak dispatch day at Haldia, 150 trucks arrive — but the ASRS loads only 90. The remaining 60 wait 6–7 hours. That is a daily detention exposure of ₹60,000 sitting on no P&L line. Across the network, 5,167 SRN credit memo lines worth ₹773.38 lakhs were processed in FY26 — yet no consolidated COPQ framework existed to link root causes to rupee impact.

🔬 Methodology

  • 6 weeks of direct field observation at Haldia Plant (M251) and Dhulagarh CFA, Kolkata (D257)
  • SAP analysis: 5,167 SRN lines × ₹773.38 lakhs across 15 depots; 9 months of expiry & near-expiry stock data = ₹130.27 lakhs (Aug 2025 – Apr 2026)
  • COPQ framework: Feigenbaum / Juran 4-quadrant model — Prevention, Appraisal, Internal Failure, External Failure costs applied to FMCG logistics for the first time
  • Truck TAT mapping, detention liability modelling, MRP mismatch exposure analysis at ASRS feed station

📊 Key Findings (Quantified)

  • Total quantified COPQ: ₹10.03 crores/year across the network
  • SRN (Stock Return Notes): ₹773.38 lakhs — e-commerce channel drives 89.2% (₹690.13 lakhs) due to quick-commerce 180-day shelf-life rule enforcement
  • Kolkata depot (D257): ₹417.87 lakhs — 54% of all-India SRN; depot SRN rate 1.60% vs best-practice target <0.5%
  • Freshness / expiry losses: ₹168.27 lakhs/year (full-year estimate)
  • Truck detention liability: ₹1.87 crores/year — dormant contractual clause; will activate at next transporter renegotiation
  • MRP mismatch at Haldia ASRS: ₹3.12 crores/year exposure across 3 plants
  • December 2025 SRN spike: ₹120.85 lakhs — 2.6× non-festive monthly average

✅ Recommendations (14 Interventions)

  • 9 of 14 interventions carry zero implementation cost
  • Move labour station down FFS conveyor (8 ft → 20–25 ft) — allows seal to harden before handling; cost ₹0
  • FEFO mandatory enforcement + batch shelf-life check before every dispatch — targets ₹168L expiry loss
  • Smart sensor installation for depot lighting & AC — ₹4.20 lakhs/year electricity saving
  • BOPT (Battery-Operated Pallet Truck) upgrade — 3-year ROI model built; ₹74.97L/year labour saving potential
  • Anti-bird net + tarpaulin covers at depot — eliminates pigeon damage and rain infiltration
  • Pre-dispatch MRP verification checklist to eliminate ₹3.12 crore ASRS mismatch exposure

First-year saving potential: ₹4.5–6.0 crores

🏆 Significance

This study builds the first consolidated COPQ map across Emami's distribution network — bringing scattered cost leakages (SRN, freshness, detention, damage) onto a single framework so the CS&L function can track and reduce wastage systematically in FY27. Pan-India net sales base: ₹2,266.68 crores (FY26); Haldia billings: ₹797.74 crores.

SAMA — Sales Return & Approval Portal

Emami Agrotech Limited · MBA Internship Project · March–May 2026

🚀 Launch SAMA Portal

🧩 Problem Statement

During the COPQ internship, SAP data revealed that 5,167 Stock Return Notes (SRNs) worth ₹773.38 lakhs were processed across Emami Agrotech's 15 depots in FY 2025–26 — with the Kolkata depot alone contributing ₹417.87 lakhs (54% of all-India SRN). The root cause was not just product quality — it was the absence of a structured, traceable return and credit note approval system. Returns were being initiated and approved through informal channels (WhatsApp, phone calls), credit notes were issued inconsistently, and there was no audit trail linking the return reason to the credit amount.

💡 Solution — SAMA Portal

SAMA (Sales Approval & Management Application) is a structured SRN portal designed to digitise the end-to-end sales return and credit note issuance workflow. The portal maps the approval hierarchy, enforces stage-gate controls, and generates credit notes automatically upon approval.

  • Sales representative submits SRN request with product details, return quantity, reason code, and distributor info
  • System routes the request to the Area Sales Manager → Regional Manager → Accounts team in sequence
  • Each approver gets a digital notification; rejections require documented reasons
  • Upon full approval, a credit note is auto-generated with reference number, amount, and GST details
  • Dashboard view for management: pending SRNs, approval TAT, rejection reasons, and credit note ledger

📊 Impact & Outcomes

  • Reduced SRN approval cycle time significantly by eliminating physical routing
  • Eliminated credit note disputes — every CN tied to a documented, approved SRN
  • Created an audit trail for all returns — traceable by product, region, sales rep, and reason code
  • Improved distributor trust and satisfaction by providing real-time status visibility on return requests

🛠️ My Role

Designed the process flow, mapped the approval hierarchy, defined data fields for each stage, and built the portal using AI-assisted design tools. Presented the solution to the supply chain and sales operations team at Emami Agrotech during the internship.
